The limit set by Vikki Bank for the Purchase of Foreign Currency in Cash service from time to time, in accordance with the regulations of the State Bank of Vietnam.

  • Vikki Bank shall process the Customer’s Purchase of Foreign Currency in Cash transactions during the Business Days prescribed by Vikki Bank.

4. Transaction Documentation Requirements

The Customer is responsible for providing documents evidencing the purpose of using foreign currency abroad, in accordance with applicable law and Vikki Bank’s current guidelines. The documentation required for each purchase purpose is as follows:
  • Payment of Overseas Tuition and Study Expenses:
    • A fee notice issued by the school or training institution showing the student’s name. Where the notice is not addressed specifically to the student, the Customer must also submit a letter of acceptance from the relevant overseas institution, or other documents evidencing enrolment abroad (e.g., student pass, Form I-20, school confirmation letter, visa, etc.); or a printout from the school’s website showing the student’s information (full name, course name, duration of study, and the institution providing the course).
    • The decision of the employing organization assigning the employee to study abroad (if applicable).
    • Passport.
    • Visa (if required by the destination country).
    • Air ticket, together with proof of payment for such ticket.
  • Payment of Overseas Medical Treatment Expenses:
    • An admission notice for examination/treatment issued by the overseas medical facility, or a referral letter for overseas treatment issued by a domestic medical facility.
    • A notice of actual or estimated costs issued by the overseas medical facility.
    • Passport.
    • Visa (if required by the destination country).
    • The patient’s air ticket, together with proof of payment for such ticket.
  • Overseas Business Trips, Tourism, or Visits:
    • An official assignment letter/dispatch (in the case of an overseas business trip).
    • A registration form for participation in an overseas tour, or confirmation from the foreign party regarding the use of services for tourism/visit purposes (if any).
    • A notice of costs for the overseas trip (if any).
    • Passport.
    • Visa (if required by the destination country).
    • Air ticket, together with proof of payment for such ticket.

5. Policies on Purchase of Foreign Currency in Cash Transactions on the Vikki App

  • Order to Purchase Cash Foreign Currency on the Vikki App: a transaction in which the Customer submits a request to purchase cash foreign currency on the Vikki App, and Vikki Bank has recorded such order as successfully placed within the timeframe prescribed by Vikki Bank from time to time. By placing such an order, the Customer authorizes Vikki Bank to temporarily hold (block) the VND amount corresponding to the quantity of foreign currency ordered.
  • Transaction Date (Date T): the date on which the Customer successfully places an order to purchase cash foreign currency on the Vikki App. The transaction agreement between the Customer and Vikki Bank is established on this date.
  • Settlement Date: the date on which Vikki Bank disburses, at its counter, the cash foreign currency ordered by the Customer on the Transaction Date. The Settlement Date must be a Business Day prescribed by Vikki Bank and must not exceed two (02) Business Days from the “Transaction Date” on the Vikki App. Where the Settlement Date falls on a weekly day off or a public holiday under Vietnamese law, it may be moved to the next Business Day.
  • Time Limit for Collecting Foreign Currency: within two (02) Business Days from the date the transaction is recorded as successful on the application.
  • Foreign Currencies Available for Transaction: as prescribed by Vikki Bank from time to time, in compliance with the regulations of the State Bank of Vietnam.
  • Exchange Rate at the Time of Placing the Order: the exchange rate determined at the time the Customer successfully places the order to purchase cash foreign currency on the Vikki App. This rate is the cash selling rate applicable to the relevant foreign currency for the Customer’s order, as prescribed by Vikki Bank from time to time and in compliance with the regulations of the State Bank of Vietnam.
  • Exchange Rate at the Time of Cancellation: the exchange rate determined at the time a transaction is successfully cancelled on the Vikki App in the circumstances set out herein, in compliance with the regulations of the State Bank of Vietnam.
IV. TRANSACTION EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

1. General Principles

  • For each air ticket and corresponding passport provided by the Customer to Vikki Bank, for which the Customer is responsible for ensuring that there is no duplication, the Customer may place an order to purchase cash foreign currency up to the maximum transaction limit prescribed by Vikki Bank from time to time.
  • When collecting cash foreign currency at the transaction counter selected by the Customer on the Vikki App, the Customer must present all documents evidencing the purpose of using such foreign currency abroad.
  • Vikki Bank may charge a foreign currency cash handling/counting fee (if applicable) in accordance with Vikki Bank’s policies from time to time, and the Customer shall be responsible for paying such fee. For the avoidance of doubt, the cash handling/counting fee is a cash service fee arising from the physical receipt and delivery of cash and does not constitute part of the foreign exchange transaction fee, exchange rate, or any foreign exchange-related charge.

2. Execution Principles

a. Cancellation of Transactions

A transaction in which the Customer has successfully purchased cash foreign currency on the Vikki App but has not yet collected such currency at the transaction counter shall be cancelled in the following cases:
  • The Customer proactively cancels the Purchase of Foreign Currency in Cash order on the Vikki App.
  • The Customer arrives to collect the cash foreign currency in accordance with the transaction, but the supporting documentation is invalid.
  • The Customer fails to collect the cash foreign currency within Vikki Bank’s working hours from Date T to Date T+2, as committed on Date T.
Penalty Amount = the VND amount held by Vikki Bank for payment of the foreign currency purchase − (quantity of foreign currency × Vikki Bank’s buy-back exchange rate at the time of cancellation), where the result is a positive amount.
Refund Amount = the amount initially held − the Penalty Amount.

b. Collection of Cash Foreign Currency

The Customer shall collect the cash foreign currency at the location selected by the Customer when successfully submitting the Purchase of Foreign Currency in Cash request on the Vikki App.
