This is a service that allows you to proactively order foreign currency online via the Vikki App if you have an international flight ticket. It is quick, flexible, and convenient. You can collect the physical cash directly at your chosen Vikki Bank branch.

(Note: The service is only available during working days from Monday to Friday from 8:30 - 16:30, not include with holiday).

Outstanding benefits when using the Vikki App include:

  • Competitive exchange rates to optimize your costs.
  • Flexible ordering time, fully operated on the Vikki App.
  • Secure and transparent transactions with a saved transaction history.
  • Easy cash collection at Vikki Bank branches nationwide.

To use this service, you must meet the following requirements:

  • Have a successfully opened VikkiME payment account from Feb 17, 2025, a GenV account, or have migrated from Vikki by HDBank to Vikki Bank.
  • Possess an international flight ticket (from Vietjet Air or other airlines). A return ticket is not strictly required.
