The Insured
Vietnamese citizens and foreigners living, studying, working, and working in Vietnam and are between the ages of 18 years old and 75 years old. The insured does not fall into the following cases:
  • Persons with a history of or currently suffering from mental illness, neurological disorders, or leprosy.
  • Persons with disability or permanent impairment of 50% or more.
Benefits and coverage
  • Sum insured: Please select an approriate plan as below.
  • Insurance period: Flexible based on customer request, up to a maximum of 12 months.
  • Beneficiary: Insured or nominated beneficiary.
  • Territory limit: Vietnam
  • Waiting period: Not Applicable
  • Insurance benefits:
  • Pay entire sum insured in case of death or total permanent disability;
  • Pay compensation subject to List of Disability Ratio attached to policy wording for permanent partial disability;
  • Pay medical expenses for temporary disability due to accident;
  • Pay allowance during hospitalization for injuries caused by accidents.
  • Please kindly refer to detailed insurance benefits of each insurance plan.
  • Beneficiary: Insured or nominated beneficiary

Exclusions
Key exclusions:
  • Intentional acts of the insured, the insurance buyer or the beneficiary (only excluding beneficiaries who commit violations);
  • The insured person violates the law on the following acts: Illegal racing; Driving a vehicle without a Driver’s License/or an invalid Driver’s License; Entering prohibited roads or restricted areas; Going in the opposite direction on a one-way street, going in the opposite direction on a road with a “Prohibited” sign; Not staying in the correct lane or designated part of the road.
  • The insured person uses alcohol or beer in excess of the concentration prescribed by the Road Traffic Law.
  • The insured person uses drugs or other stimulants.
  • The insured person dies suddenly or of unknown cause or dies without being able to determine the cause of death for any reason.
Other exclusions are specifically specified in the Insurance Wording.
